How do critical hits work?
On a natural 20, the attack hits automatically. Roll all damage dice twice, then add modifiers once.

How does multiattack work?
Extra Attack lets you attack multiple times when you take the Attack action. Each is a separate roll.

How does opportunity attack work?
When an enemy leaves your reach without Disengaging, you can use your reaction to make one melee attack.
