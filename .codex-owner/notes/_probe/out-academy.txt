How do I make an attack roll?
Roll d20 + ability modifier + proficiency bonus (if proficient). Meet or beat the target's AC to hit.
Attack
How do critical hits work?
On a natural 20, the attack hits automatically. Roll all damage dice twice, then add modifiers once.
Attack
How does multiattack work?
Extra Attack lets you attack multiple times when you take the Attack action. Each is a separate roll.
Attack
How does opportunity attack work?
When an enemy leaves your reach without Disengaging, you can use your reaction to make one melee attack.
Attack
How do saving throws work?
Roll d20 + ability modifier + proficiency bonus (if proficient). Meet or beat the DC to resist the effect.
Saving Throw
What is an ability check?
Roll d20 + ability modifier + proficiency bonus (if proficient in the relevant skill). Meet or beat the DC.
Ability Check
How does advantage/disadvantage work?
Roll 2d20. With advantage, take the higher. With disadvantage, take the lower. They cancel each other out.
Ability Check
What is proficiency bonus?
A bonus that scales with your total character level: +2 at levels 1-4, increasing to +6 at levels 17-20.
Ability Check
What happens at 0 HP?
You fall unconscious and start making death saving throws at the start of each turn.
Damage
How do death saves work?
Roll d20 at the start of your turn: 10+ is a success, 9 or below is a failure. 3 successes = stable, 3 failures = death.
Damage
How does healing work?
Healing restores HP up to your maximum. It cannot exceed max HP. Healing from 0 HP makes you conscious again.
Damage
What is temporary HP?
Extra buffer HP that absorbs damage first. Does not stack (use highest). Cannot be healed. Lasts until depleted or rest.
Damage
How do conditions work?
Conditions impose specific mechanical effects. They last until cured, dispelled, or their duration ends.
Conditions
How does grappling work?
Use an attack to grab: your Athletics vs. their Athletics or Acrobatics. Success sets their speed to 0.
Conditions
How does cover work?
Half cover: +2 AC/DEX saves. Three-quarters cover: +5 AC/DEX saves. Total cover: Cannot be targeted directly.
Conditions
What are the action types in combat?
Each turn: 1 Action + 1 Bonus Action + 1 Reaction + Movement. Free interactions (open door, draw weapon) also available.
Action Economy
How do bonus actions work?
You get one per turn, but only if a feature or spell explicitly grants one. Cannot replace an action.
Action Economy
How do reactions work?
One per round (resets at your turn start). Triggered by a specific event. Common: opportunity attack, Shield, Counterspell.
Action Economy
What is the Help action?
Give one ally advantage on their next ability check or attack roll against a target within 5 ft of you.
Action Economy
How does Dodge action work?
Until your next turn, attacks against you have disadvantage and you have advantage on DEX saves. Ends if incapacitated or speed drops to 0.
Action Economy
How does concentration work?
Only one concentration spell at a time. Take damage = CON save (DC 10 or half damage, whichever is higher) or lose it.
Spellcasting
