BASIC ACTIONS — THE RULES 
The rules-of-the-game actions anyone can take — they are on no character sheet, so they are not in your turn list. Tap one to ask the advisor about it. 
Attack 
Action 
Make one melee or ranged attack (or more with Extra Attack). 
Cast a Spell 
Action 
Cast a spell with a casting time of 1 Action. 
Dash 
Action 
Double your movement speed for this turn. 
Disengage 
Action 
Your movement doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks for the rest of the turn. 
Dodge 
Action 
Attack rolls against you have Disadvantage, and you have Advantage on DEX saves until your next turn. 
Help 
Action 
Give an ally Advantage on their next ability check or attack roll against a target within 5ft of you. 
Hide 
Action 
Make a Stealth check to become Hidden. 
Influence 
Action 
Make a Charisma check to influence a creature's attitude. 
Magic 
Action 
Use a magic item or interact with a magical effect. 
Ready 
Action 
Prepare an action to trigger on a specific condition (uses your Reaction). 
Search 
Action 
Make a Perception or Investigation check to find something. 
Study 
Action 
Make a check to recall or discern information. 
Utilize 
Action 
Use a non-magical object or interact with the environment.

level 3 · your sheet 
The cloak is activated 'As a Reaction' but no trigger is defined. 2024 requires every Reaction to state its trigger: 'A Reaction is a special action taken in response to a trigger defined in the Reaction's description.' Without a trigger the feature cannot be legally used.

HEARTH-04 
level 3 · your sheet 
The cloak lasts 'until the Temporary Hit Points are depleted'. In 2024 Temporary Hit Points DO NOT STACK - when you gain new Temporary Hit Points you choose to keep the old pool or take the new one. Taking any other source of Temporary Hit Points (Heroism, Inspiring Leader, Orc Adrenaline Rush, a Chef treat, Aid does not apply) REPLACES the cloak's pool, which by the feature's own wording ends the cloak.
