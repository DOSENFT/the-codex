→ THE CIRCLE
LIVE
USE IT WHEN
The party has just entered a relatively quiet, slightly rundown tavern. There's a low hum of conversation and the smell of stale ale.
THE MOVE
DO
Nix scans the room, subtly mimicking the slumped posture of a weary-looking dwarf nursing a drink in the corner. He then walks to the bar, placing his shield against the counter with a soft thud.
SAY
Strong ale. Something that tastes like it's seen a few seasons.
ASK
Anything interesting happen here lately, barkeep?
WHAT IT’S FOR
This beat establishes Nix's observant nature and subtly hints at his changeling ability (mimicry). It also opens a line of inquiry to learn about the local area and potential problems, fitting his protective nature. It's a low-pressure opening that invites information.
IF THEY BITE
If the barkeep responds with a story, ask follow-up questions about the details. If the barkeep is gruff, offer to buy a round for the whole bar to loosen their tongues.
