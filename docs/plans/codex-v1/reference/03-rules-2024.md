**So this is not an edition migration. It is a correction to my Gate-1 mockups, which I seeded with
2014 assumptions.** Good news: the expensive work was already done. Bad news: the ranked shortlist —
the spine of the redesign — was ranking on the wrong action economy.

⚠ One trunk gap: **"Bloodied" is not implemented** (zero occurrences in `src/`). It is a formally
defined 2024 term and several effects trigger on it. Added to the fix list.
⚠ One trunk inconsistency: the field is `race: string` (`character.ts:216`) while the 2024 term is
**species**. Cosmetic, but it is the kind of thing that quietly teaches the AI the wrong vocabulary.

## The six rule facts that change the turn screen

### 1. Divine Smite is a Bonus Action spell, once per turn — not a free rider

2014: a class feature. No action cost, no per-turn cap, declared *after* seeing the hit land, stackable
on every attack in the turn.

2024: **a level 1 Evocation spell.** *Casting Time: Bonus Action, which you take immediately after
hitting a target with a Melee weapon or an Unarmed Strike.* Verbal component. 2d8 radiant, +1d8 vs
Fiend or Undead, +1d8 per slot level above 1. **The 5d8 cap is gone.**

Two independent limits make it once-per-turn: one Bonus Action per turn, and *"On a turn, you can
expend only one spell slot to cast a spell."*

**Paladin's Smite (level 2):** Divine Smite is always prepared, and **one cast per long rest costs no
slot.** That free cast is a distinct, trackable resource — and because it spends no slot, RAW it does
not consume the one-slot-per-turn budget. It still costs the Bonus Action.

Because it is a spell with a **V component**, it can be Counterspelled, and a *Silence*d or gagged
paladin cannot smite. The turn screen must be able to grey it out for that reason.

⚠ Unresolved RAW: *"hitting with a Melee weapon"* is a weapon-**category** test, so a thrown dagger
arguably qualifies. No WotC errata resolves it. Make it a setting, not a hardcoded rule.

### 2. Lay on Hands is now a Bonus Action — so it fights Divine Smite

2014: an Action. 2024: **a Bonus Action.** Pool still 5 × level (**40 at level 8**). Now explicitly
*"which could be yourself."* Disease-curing removed; 5 points now removes the **Poisoned condition**
specifically. The 2014 "no effect on undead and constructs" exclusion is gone.

**This is the central turn-planning tension of a 2024 Paladin and the screen must model it as a mutex:
Smite, Lay on Hands, and any bonus-action spell all compete for one slot in the economy.**

### 3. Vow of Enmity is free — a rider on the Attack action

| | 2014 | 2024 |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | **Bonus Action** | **Free** — *"When you take the Attack action, you can expend one use of your Channel Divinity"* |
| Range | 10 ft | **30 ft** |
| Ends | when target hits 0 HP | 1 minute, or when you reuse it |
| On target death | advantage lost | **transfers to a new target within 30 ft, no action** |

They swapped: in 2014 Smite was free and Vow cost the bonus action. In 2024 it is the reverse. A
level-8 Vengeance Paladin can now Attack ×2 **+ Vow of Enmity + Divine Smite** in one turn.

The screen must never offer Vow as a bonus action, and must prompt the free re-target when a vowed
enemy drops.

### 4. Channel Divinity: 2 uses, and a short rest returns only one

2024: gained at level 3, **2 uses** (3 at level 11). *"You regain one of its expended uses when you
finish a Short Rest, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest."* Save DC = the
Paladin spell save DC. Divine Sense is now a Channel Divinity **option** (Bonus Action, 10 minutes),
not a separate level-1 feature.

For a Vengeance Paladin the CD menu is exactly two items: **Divine Sense** and **Vow of Enmity**.
Abjure Enemy was deleted.

### 5. Weapon Mastery — every attack now carries a rider

New in 2024, fully in the SRD. Paladin gets it at level 1: **2 weapons, never increases**, and unlike
Barbarian/Fighter the Paladin may swap **all** its choices on a long rest.

**Longsword = Sap.** *"If you hit a creature with this weapon, that creature has Disadvantage on its
next attack roll before the start of your next turn."*

Implementation notes that matter to the UI:
- **No mastery costs an action.** Nick is the only one that touches the economy, and it *gives* economy back.
- **Sap and Vex are automatic** ("that creature *has* Disadvantage") — auto-apply them. The other six
  say "you can" — prompt.
- **Topple is the only one with a save** (Con, DC 8 + the ability mod used for that attack + PB).
- **Slow and Vex require damage to land**; Push/Sap/Topple/Cleave need only a hit.
- Sap and Vex create **cross-turn state** with *different windows*: Sap is "before the start of your
  next turn," Vex is "before the end of your next turn." The tracker must expire them separately.

Full table for reference: greatsword *Graze* · warhammer *Push* · rapier & shortsword *Vex* ·
battleaxe & maul & quarterstaff *Topple* · greataxe & halberd *Cleave* · mace & spear & flail & war
pick & morningstar *Sap* · dagger & scimitar & light hammer & sickle *Nick* · javelin & club & whip &
longbow *Slow*.

### 6. Bloodied is a real term again, and it needs edge detection

*"A creature is Bloodied while it has half its Hit Points or fewer remaining"* — so `hp <= floor(max/2)`,
and exactly half **is** Bloodied. **It is not a condition** (absent from the condition list); it is a
status flag with no intrinsic effect that other effects trigger on. Several 2024 triggers read
"**becomes** Bloodied," so the tracker needs edge detection, not just a boolean.

## Secondary changes the app must respect (not turn-screen-shaping, but wrong if ignored)

- **Incapacitated is now a heavy composite** — no action/bonus action/reaction, **Concentration broken**,
  can't speak, Disadvantage on Initiative. Paralyzed, Petrified, Stunned and Unconscious all *include*
  it. **Model it as a composable sub-condition and cascade it**, don't hardcode per parent condition.
  It also switches off Aura of Protection.
- **Aura of Protection** is a 10-ft **Emanation** (a defined 2024 area term), inactive while
  Incapacitated (2014 said "must be conscious" — narrower). New: two Paladins' auras don't stack.
- **Surprise no longer costs a turn.** It is now only **Disadvantage on the Initiative roll**. The
  entire 2014 "can't move or act on your first turn" rule is gone.
- **Concentration**: same Con save, DC = max(10, half damage), now capped at **DC 30**. Broken *"the
  moment you start casting"* another concentration effect — so it breaks even if the new spell is
  Counterspelled. ⚠ The 2014 "separate save per damage source" sentence is **absent** from SRD 5.2.1
  and nothing replaced it. Prompt one save per source as the default, but expose it as a setting.
- **Opportunity Attack** dropped the word "hostile" (allies can provoke) and is explicitly *"with a
  weapon or an Unarmed Strike"* — which, with the Unarmed Strike change, means **an OA can now be a
  Grapple or a Shove**.
- **Unarmed Strike** is one strike with three options — Damage, Grapple, or Shove. Grapple/Shove are
  now a **saving throw against a static DC (8 + Str + PB)**, the target choosing Str or Dex. The
  attacker rolls nothing. This is not a contested check any more.
- **Exhaustion** is a flat **−2 × level on every D20 Test** and −5 ft × level Speed; death at level 6.
  Because it is numeric it now **stacks with Advantage/Disadvantage** instead of being swallowed.
- **Hide** grants the **Invisible condition** on a DC 15 Stealth check. Invisible is now dual-purpose —
  do not apply Hide's four break-triggers to spell-derived invisibility.
- **Stunned no longer sets Speed to 0.** **Unconscious ending leaves you Prone** — don't auto-clear it.
- **Action list is 12**, not 10: Influence and Study are new; Magic and Utilize are renames; Search is
  now Wisdom-only (Investigation moved to Study, so **trap-finding defaults to Perception**).
- **Two cantrips in one turn is now legal** — the limit is one *slot* per turn, not one spell.
- **Frightened did not change.** Same two clauses, same line-of-sight asymmetry. My mockups were right.
- **Restrained, Blinded, Deafened, Poisoned, death saves, massive damage, temp HP** — unchanged.
- **Species** replaced Race, grant **no ability score increases** (those moved to backgrounds), and are
  **not level-independent** — Dragonborn/Elf/Tiefling/Goliath all gain traits at levels 3–5, so the
  character model must track level for species traits.
- **Backgrounds** grant +2/+1 or +1/+1/+1, an **Origin feat**, two skills, one tool, and equipment-or-50gp.

## What this does to the ranked shortlist — the reason it matters

Under 2014 economics my shortlist ranked: Oathkeeper → Divine Smite (free) → Misty Step → Lay on
Hands → Vow of Enmity (spent). Smite sat at #2 because it was free.

Under 2024 economics the correct ranking is different in kind, because **the bonus action is now
contested and the slot budget is one per turn**:

1. **Vow of Enmity** — costs *nothing*. Free rider on the Attack action, and its Advantage **cancels
   the Frightened disadvantage**. It is first precisely because it is free.
2. **Oathkeeper ×2** — the Action. +8 to hit, 1d8+5, and **Sap** on each hit.
3. **Divine Smite** — the Bonus Action. The free cast is unspent, so this turn it costs **no slot**.
4. **Lay on Hands 15** — *same bonus action*. Mutually exclusive with 3.
5. **Misty Step** — *same bonus action, and the turn's only slot*. Excluded twice over.

Items 3, 4 and 5 are not five independent choices — they are **one choice with three faces**, and the
screen has to say so. That is a design requirement the 2014 version of this screen never had, and it
is the single strongest argument for grouping the shortlist by economy rather than by raw rank.
