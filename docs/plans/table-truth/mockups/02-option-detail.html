What it does

Immediately after you hit with a melee weapon or an Unarmed Strike, you spend your Bonus Action to channel 2d8 extra Radiant damage into that hit, plus another 1d8 if the target is a Fiend or Undead.

⚑ One slot per turn

You have not spent a slot this turn, so this is legal. If you cast Faerie Fire with your Action, this greys out — 2024 allows one slot expended per turn, total. Your free Paladin's Smite (1 per long rest) expends none, so it is exempt.

How to use it ▾
  • Smite after you see the roll, never before. The casting time is "immediately after hitting" — you never spend a slot on a miss.
  • With Extra Attack, sequence it. Take the first swing. If it crits, Smite now. If it only hits, hold and take the second swing — let the dice pick which hit carries it.
  • Smite every crit, no hesitation. A crit doubles all the attack's damage dice, Smite dice included. A 2nd-level Smite on a crit is 6d8.
  • Priority: concentrating enemy casters first (the damage forces a save at DC 10 or half), then Fiends and Undead (+1d8), then anything about to drop.

Four bands, always the same four, in the same order. Stat block → what it does → the rolls → how to use it. That fixed order is what makes it a "quick glance" instead of a wall — the eye learns where the range lives and stops reading.

Nothing here is generated at the table. The summary, the stat block and the tactics all come from the canon file, shipped with the app. It works with the AI off, the wifi off, and the tunnel down.

The rule box is live, not decorative. It reads the current turn — it says "you have not spent a slot this turn" because that is true right now, and it is the single most-broken rule in D&D apps (CORRECTIONS.md §1).

"How to use it" is folded by default. It is long, and it is not the answer to "what does this do". Answering the fast question fast is the whole point.
