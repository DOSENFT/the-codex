As your subclass is written

"Whenever you use a Magic action to cast Divine Smite, add 1d8 Fire damage to each smite. This Fire damage ignores Resistance and Immunity to Fire damage."

Why it does nothing

In 2024, Divine Smite's casting time is "Bonus Action, taken immediately after hitting a target". It is never a Magic action. The trigger can never be satisfied, so the feature has no effect at any point in the game.

Likely cause: written against 2014 rules, where Divine Smite was a class feature rather than a spell.

Also worth knowing

"Ignores Resistance and Immunity" is stronger than any official feature — Elemental Adept, Poisoner and even the level 19 Boon of Irresistible Offense ignore Resistance only. At 1d8 it is not game-breaking, but your DM should know it is a deliberate step above curve. (HEARTH-09)
