Tapping “Level Up” — 8 → 9

Nix at Charisma 16 (+3). Level 9 is the next boundary where proficiency moves (+3 → +4), so it’s the clearest look at what the button does and doesn’t do. Values that should have moved and didn’t are orange.

“Update your spells and features as needed” is the app asking him to do arithmetic it already has the table for — and not telling him which four numbers are now wrong. His save DC stays a point low until he notices.

Level 9. Proficiency +3 → +4, so your save DC is now 15 and your spell attack +7. Lay on Hands 40 → 45. You can prepare 9 spells, up from 7. Your max HP, AC and spell slots are yours to set.

Save DC and spell attack aren’t “updated” — there is nothing to update. They’re worked out from Charisma and level whenever they’re shown, so they were never anything else.

Spell slots deliberately stay his. The rules would add 3rd-level slots here, but his sheet already disagrees with the table at level 7, and that may be his DM or an item. The app flags it and leaves it alone.
