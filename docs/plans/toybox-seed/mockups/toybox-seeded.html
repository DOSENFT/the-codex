The Reaction Is Only One criticalcore
When: Hearthfire is up, an enemy turn is starting, and you have three different things that all want your reaction.
  1. Decide before their turn begins. Deciding during it is how you freeze the table.
  2. Cloak if the attention is on you — 10 temp HP eats a hit and answers 1d10 fire.
  3. Sentinel if an ally is the target — your swing also drops the attacker's speed to 0, which is worth more than the damage.
  4. Interception if the hit already landed and it is the damage that matters — reduce it by 1d10+3.
  5. You get one. Not two. Pick the default now so the table isn't waiting on you.
Hearthfire Manifest active · Channel Divinity available · fighting style: Interception

Stand Where the Aura Pays criticalcore
When: anything is about to force saving throws — a caster is spotted, a trap trips, something inhales.
  1. Aura of Protection is a 10-foot radius, and it is always on. It costs nothing.
  2. +3 to every save, for everyone inside it. That is larger than most things you could do with your whole action.
  3. Aura of Solace stacks on top: resistance to Fire, Cold and Psychic inside the same radius.
  4. So against a fire breath, or a mind-flayer type, moving one square beats every spell on your list.
Rune and Talon are the low-constitution ones. If the radius only reaches two people, it reaches those two.
None. This is on whether you remember it or not — which is exactly why it is written down.

Preparing for Tomorrow criticalcore
When: you finish a long rest. You may swap exactly one prepared spell.
  1. Your four oath spells — Faerie Fire, Burning Hands, Scorching Ray, Warding Bond — are always prepared and do not count. Neither do Divine Smite or Find Steed.
  2. So all seven picks are genuinely yours. You are currently using two: Bless and Shield of Faith. Five are sitting empty.
  3. Expect a boss with big saves? Keep Bless, add Compelled Duel — it forces the fight to be about you, which is the fight you win.
  4. Expect a swarm? Burning Hands is already free. Spend a pick on Wrathful Smite instead — frightened enemies cannot close, and your reach punishes that.
  5. Expect a long day with no healer? Cure Wounds and Aid. Aid raises the whole party's maximum, not just their current.
