Canon's own words, with your numbers filled in.

The standard ranged answer for a Blessed Warrior Paladin, and the choice between this and Toll the Dead is the real decision.
It ignores cover
— that is Sacred Flame's unique selling point and it is genuinely valuable. An archer behind a wall, a caster peeking around a corner: cover normally gives +2 or +5 to Dexterity saves and AC. Sacred Flame ignores it entirely.
