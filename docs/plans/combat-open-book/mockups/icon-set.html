Art direction — the rules these have to obey.
  • Silhouette carries it. At 34px on a phone in a lit room, only the outline survives. One shape per mark, no scene, no background detail.
  • Colour groups by what it does, not what it is. Ember = it hurts something. Verdant = it keeps someone up. Arcane gold = divine / protective. Bronze = mundane weapon or positioning.
  • No mark may outshout the number. The mark is a finding aid; the hero die is still the loudest thing on the card.
  • They live inside the frame we already have — 9px radius at row size, 16px at card size, the same 1px arcane hairline every other surface uses. New art, not a new visual language.
  • Twenty, not eighty-six. The combat kit only. The Grimoire's long tail stays text-only until these prove out.
